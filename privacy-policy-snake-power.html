Privacy Policy

Snake Power: Wall Breaker

Effective date: 15 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Snake Power: Wall Breaker (“the Game”), developed and published by the developer identified on its Google Play listing, handles information when you use the Game.

1. Information we collect

The Game does not require you to create an account and, in its current version, does not ask you to submit personal information such as your name, phone number, postal address, or email address in order to play.

Gameplay information such as game progress, scores, coins, and unlocked items may be stored locally on your device so that the Game can remember your progress. This information is stored on your device and is not transmitted to a server operated by the developer by the Game's current functionality.

2. How information is used

Locally stored gameplay information is used only to provide game features, remember progress, and improve your playing experience. The developer does not use this local gameplay information to identify you.

3. Sharing and sale of information

The developer does not sell or share personal information collected by the Game with third parties. The current version of the Game does not include advertising or analytics services that collect information for the developer.

4. Third-party services and platform information

The Game is distributed through Google Play. Google and your device or operating-system provider may process information under their own privacy policies when you download or use the Game or related platform services. The developer does not control those independent practices.

5. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored gameplay data remains on your device until it is deleted by the Game, you clear the Game's storage, or you uninstall the Game. Uninstalling the Game or clearing its storage may remove locally saved progress.

6. Children's privacy

The Game does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to the developer, please contact us using the email address below so that we can review the concern.

7. Security

Because gameplay information is stored locally on your device, you should protect your device and use its available security features. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

8. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the Game's features or data practices change. The updated version will be published at the same Privacy Policy URL, with a revised effective date.
